Echoes of a Lost World

Long before humans walked the Earth, our planet was home to extraordinary creatures that lived in worlds very different from our own. From the frozen landscapes of the Ice Age to the ancient forests ruled by massive dinosaurs, these remarkable species shaped the history of life on Earth. Some disappeared because of dramatic changes in climate and their environment, while others faced competition, natural disasters, or the changing conditions of their world. Today, their stories survive through fossils, scientific discoveries, and the traces they left behind. Paleora takes you on a journey through these lost worlds, bringing extinct species back to life through their stories, habitats, and fascinating history.
